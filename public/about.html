About me

Hi, I'm Yash.
I tell wedding stories.

I'm Yash Sanjay Daware, the photographer and filmmaker behind The Cre8ive YD. I'm from Kalyan, Mumbai, and I've been shooting weddings since 2020.

Since then I've covered more than 200 weddings: big celebrations and small family functions, haldi mornings and late-night sangeets. Each one taught me the same thing. The best moments aren't posed. They happen between the rituals, and my job is to be ready for them.

I bring together candid photography, cinematic films and aerial drone coverage, so your whole day is told by one team with one eye for the story. I travel for weddings all over India and abroad too, wherever your celebration takes us.
