The studio

Creative by name.
Honest by nature.

The Cre8ive YD is a photography and videography studio from Kalyan, Mumbai. We document weddings and celebrations the way they actually feel — the nervous laughter before the pheras, the dance floor at 1 a.m., the quiet look across a crowded mandap.

Stills, cinematic films and aerial drone footage, captured by one team with one vision. No stiff poses, no assembly-line albums — just your day, told beautifully.

What we shoot

Services, start to finish

  1. 01

    Wedding Photography

    Candid and traditional coverage across every ritual — haldi, mehendi, sangeet, pheras and reception — delivered as a fully edited gallery and premium album.

  2. 02

    Cinematic Wedding Films

    A 3–5 minute highlight film, a full-length documentary edit and quick social teasers, graded and scored to feel like a movie about you.

  3. 03

    Pre-Wedding Shoots

    Concept-led photo and video sessions at locations around Mumbai and beyond — beaches, forts, hills or the café where it all started.

  4. 04

    Events & Celebrations

    Engagements, birthdays, baby showers, anniversaries and corporate events — documented with the same care as a wedding.

  5. 05

    Aerial Drone Coverage

    Sweeping venue reveals, baraat processions from above and dramatic couple shots — available as an add-on or on its own.
